# MintPlayer.Spark.ReferenceData.Sources.Belgium

The Belgian reference-data adapter (docs/reference_data_PRD.md §4.4): Statbel REFNIS (units since 1970), Statbel NIS6 (sections) and BeST postal streets (postal localities and streets), mapped with lineage derived without curation, the Belgian country profile, the id ledger and real sample fixtures.

Fixtures (where present) are real samples cut from the publisher's files; their licences and credit lines are in the source classes and at `/api/v1/sources`.

## Fixtures (real samples, RS8; cut 2026-10-19)

| File | Cut from | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `TU_COM_REFNIS-20250101.cut.txt` | Statbel `TU_COM_REFNIS-20250101.zip` | every region and province row; the arrondissements and municipalities of the seed (with the municipalities that ended in 1976 and match a seeded section by name); bytes kept (cp1252, CRLF) |
| `NIS6withnamefrom01012025.cut.xlsx` | Statbel `NIS6withnamefrom01012025.xlsx` | the sections of the seeded municipalities; the workbook rebuilt with the same parts, shared strings re-indexed |
| `{Region}_postal_street.cut.csv` | BeST `postalstreets-latest.zip` (2026-10-18) | for each (postcode, locality) of a seeded municipality with at least 3 streets: the first 3 by name plus one "kerk"/"église" street; raw lines kept (quoting quirks, BOM) |

No bpost data (RD-DEF 9). Ledger: `Ledger/be.ndjson`, regenerated by `--refdata-build-seed` (see docs/guide-reference-data.md).
