# MintPlayer.Spark.ReferenceData.Sources.Holidays

Public holidays for MintPlayer.Spark reference data (commerce GC11): statutory national holidays of the seeded countries computed from their statutes (fixed dates and the Easter cycle), as PublicHoliday documents for commerce's cut-offs and delivery promises.

Fixtures (where present) are real samples cut from the publisher's files; their licences and credit lines are in the source classes and at `/api/v1/sources`.
